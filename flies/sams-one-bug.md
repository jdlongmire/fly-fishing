# Sams One Bug

**Type:** Popper (surface / topwater foam fly)
**Imitates:** Nothing in particular. A durable attractor bug for warmwater surface feeders.
**Sizes I carry:** #6 (classic recipe size)
**Colors:** Yellow and orange (classic, as on Wade Blevins's card)

## Origin

Developed in the late 1980s by Craig "Sam" Blevins for his bass fishing. He wanted one surface fly tough enough to survive predatory fish and float well, and the published account credits a single fly with more than 100 fish. Source: [Ahrex, Sam's One Bug tied by Soren Flarup](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6u-QVAP9Ig).

## Captured at

Project Healing Waters, Huntsville chapter tying session, Tuesday October 13, 2026, at The Shop HSV, Huntsville, AL. Demoed and taught by Wade Blevins, son of the fly's creator, Sam Blevins.

Contact on his card: samsonebug@gmail.com, #samsonebug.

## When it produces

Warmwater season, spring and summer especially. Bass and panfish (bream / bluegill) around shore, cover, and structure, when fish will come up for a surface bug.

## How to fish it

Cast it in tight to shore, cover, or structure. Let it sit until the ripples fade, then give it a small twitch or pop and let it sit again. Slow pulls and short hops also work. Takes are visual and often violent, so stay ready at the pop.

## Tying notes

Published recipe (Ahrex tying by Soren Flarup, linked above; not yet verified against Wade's session recipe):

- Hook: Ahrex Light Stinger NS122 #6
- Thread: Fluorescent orange
- Tail: Gold Crystal Flash, plus yellow and orange marabou
- Body: Yellow foam cylinder, 5/16 in (8 mm)
- Legs: Silicone micro mini legs, barred black on fluorescent orange
- Wing: Red fly foam, 2 mm
- Eyes: Fluorescent orange and black UV resin

Visible on the card photo: yellow foam body, orange rubber legs, big black eyes ringed in orange, orange foam wing, yellow marabou tail.

Session materials above are as captured live; anything else Wade taught that night can be added here.

## Session notes from Wade's demo

Captured live at the session, Oct 13, 2026:

- **Body foam: "Live Body" foam.** Wade named Live Body foam for the body. That is Wapsi's Live-Body foam, sold as foam cylinders (and blocks): a soft foam used for popper and surface bodies, which can be shaped and colored with markers or acrylic paint. Product reference: [Stone River Outfitters, Large Foam Cylinders](https://www.stoneriveroutfitters.com/fly-tying/lrg-foam-cylinders-black.html).
- **Hook: #6 nymph hook.** Wade prefers a #6 nymph hook for this fly.
- **Adhesive: Gorilla Glue gel.** Wade likes Gorilla Glue gel (the gel super glue) at the bench for this tie.
- **Tool tip: spring-loaded pliers.** Use spring-loaded pliers to squeeze and hold the foam while the glue sets.
- **Coloring: neon orange Sharpie.** Wade coats the indicator foam (the top foam piece) with a neon orange Sharpie.
- **Legs: medium round rubber legs.** Fluorescent orange, or colored to match the fly style. Wade prefers the front legs a little bit longer than the rear legs.
