# Jack Daniels Micro-Flash

**Type:** Nymph (beadhead, scud hook)
**Imitates:** Mayfly larva (micro adaptation of the Jack Daniels nymph)
**Sizes I carry:** #18, #20, #22
**Colors:** Tan body with black marker segments; hybrid variant in olive D-Rib

## When it produces

Not yet recorded. First tied Nov 20, 2026 and not yet fished. The parent pattern is a Euro nymph known as a confidence fly on pressured water; this micro version is aimed at small, clear, heavily fished water.

## How to fish it

Dead drift it on a tight line or under an indicator, like other small beadhead nymphs. (Standard practice for the type; JD's own results are not yet logged.)

## Tying notes

JD's adaptation, first tied Nov 20, 2026, of the Jack Daniels nymph by Cory Cuje (Old Dominion Trout Bum). The original is a Euro nymph: jig hook, slotted tungsten copper bead, tan thread body (UTC 70 tan), black marker for the ribbing, a UV resin coat (Loon Flow), a short Coq de Leon tail, and a CDC and peacock dubbing collar. Sources: Trout Strike, "The Jack Daniels Nymph is the Ultimate Confidence Bug" and "Top 12 Euro Nymphs" (troutstrike.com).

JD kept the body formula and shrank the rest onto the Green Machine chassis:

- Hook: Scud hook, barbed, sizes 18, 20, and 22
- Bead: Copper, brass; same bead run as the Green Machine (5/64 in on the #18; 1/16 in on the #20 and #22)
- Body: Tan thread body; black Sharpie applied to the thread to call out the segments
- Flash: Wrapped over the body (the micro-Flash change; the original carries no wrapped flash in the body)
- Coat: UV resin over the finished body
- No D-Rib, no tail, and no collar in the base version

## Variants

- **Olive D-Rib hybrid (Nov 20, 2026):** tan thread and flash as above, with olive D-Rib wrapped over the body; black Sharpie used to color the thread for the rib and for the collar; JD's custom dub mix applied with a dubbing loop as a collar behind the bead. Bench note: tease the collar fibers out and pinch the longest strays back to about body length, so the collar reads as legs, not a halo.
