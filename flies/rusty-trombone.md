# Rusty Trombone

**Type:** Streamer (articulated, two-hook)
**Imitates:** Baitfish
**Sizes I carry:** #4 (recipe size, front and rear hooks)
**Colors:** Olive and copper

## Origin

The Rusty Trombone is the olive-with-copper-flash colorway of the Articulated Goldie, an articulated streamer that is a go-to pattern in Northern Colorado and Wyoming. The base Goldie is white and gold; other colorways mentioned by the source include black with red flash and grey with silver flash. Source: [DiscountFlies, Tying the Articulated Goldie and Rusty Trombone Fishing Flies](https://www.discountflies.com/blog/tying-the-articulate-goldie-and-rusty-trombone-fishing-flies/).

## When it produces

A searching streamer for trout water where baitfish are on the menu. The jointed two-hook body gives it a swimming action that a single-hook streamer does not.

## How to fish it

Cast and strip it, or swing it, like other articulated streamers: vary the retrieve, pause between strips, and expect takes on the pause or the first strip after it.

## Tying notes

Recipe (DiscountFlies, linked above):

- Hook: Streamer hook, size 4, for rear and front hooks
- Cone: Raven and Wolf 7.7mm tungsten conehead, copper or matte black
- Thread: UTC 140 denier, olive
- Tail and wing: Marabou, olive
- Flash: Hedron Flashabou, copper
- Body 1: SEMPERFLI Synthetic Crystal Marabou, brown olive
- Body 2: Cascade Crest Streamer Hackle, olive
- Wire (articulation): Spyder Wire Braid, 40 lb, moss green
- Collar: Cascade Crest Schlappen, olive

Tying notes from the source: the two body materials can be wrapped forward three ways, from least to most durable - wrapped sequentially, twisted together into a rope first, or spun together in a dubbing loop. For the most durable articulation, push the wire ends through the cone, down through the hook eye, and back through the cone head, secure with thread, and add superglue before wrapping over the braid ends.

The flash is the material JD asked about on Oct 21, 2026: the small flat flashy ribbon in this pattern is copper Hedron Flashabou.
